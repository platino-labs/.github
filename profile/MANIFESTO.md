# Manifesto

**Platino Labs · public position · 2026-11-01 · v1.0**

What we require of our own claims — and therefore of every audit we sell.

## 1. A number without its locus is text

Every figure we publish arrives with the artifact that produced it, the version it was measured on, and the command that reproduces it. If we cannot name the file, the line and the run, we do not call it a result; we call it an impression.

This is not modesty. It is the only way a reader can tell a finding from a preference.

## 2. Five rules we apply to ourselves

| Rule | What it demands | What breaks it |
|---|---|---|
| **R1** | The prediction is fixed before the run | A falsifier whose trigger is impossible by construction |
| **R2** | The instrument has a negative control | A green result with no fixture proving the instrument can go red |
| **R3** | An identity anchor: equal cardinality when comparing | An advantage explained by size, reported as a finding |
| **R4** | The cut is declared in advance, not chosen afterwards | A figure without its edge — the threshold, the floor, the `j` |
| **R5** | Every sentence carries artifact + version + exit code | A sentence without a record |

The rules mention no domain, model, language or substrate. That is the point: a trigger that cannot fire is impossible in any field, and a control that does not isolate identity isolates nothing anywhere.

## 3. The ontological firewall

We separate what kind of claim we are making, because a verbal resemblance is not an identity between domains.

| Domain | What may be asserted | What does not follow without further evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Mathematical / empirical | A numerical or topological relation measured under a declared definition, sample and null | That it is universal, intentional, or a law of the cosmos |
| Engineering | The behaviour of an implementation: inputs, transformation, invariants, tests | That a spiritual theory justifies the design, or that code validates it |
| Historical / philosophical | What a tradition or author proposes, with sources identified | That the proposal is a physical mechanism or a confirmed scientific theory |
| Editorial | An interpretation explicitly presented as analogy | That metaphorical language functions as a measurement |

The branches of our research can be compared through explicit questions. They do not fuse into one vocabulary. "Not measured" means neither "true" nor "false" nor "absent".

## 4. What this is not

- **Not a certification.** A structural metric is a signal to investigate, not proof of security, quality or runtime behaviour.
- **Not a promise of absence.** A clean credential scan is not proof that no credential exists.
- **Not a theory of everything.** Where we publish an analogy, it is labelled as one.
- **Not free.** "Sustainable" does not mean cost-free; when we speak about energy we do it only with a declared boundary, load, comparator and baseline.

## 5. Where the method comes from

The lab began by measuring numerical models — dynamics of a double pendulum, transport thresholds, nodal interpolation error — where a result is only as good as its declared grid, tolerance and comparator. The same discipline then moved to software structure: dependency graphs, import cycles, AST extraction.

Those internal records are not published here. What is published is the standard they imposed.

## 6. What you can hold us to

If a claim in any of our repositories cannot be reproduced with the command that accompanies it, that claim is wrong and we will withdraw it. Open an issue in the repository concerned, or reach us at <https://github.com/platino-labs>.
